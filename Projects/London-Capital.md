# London Capital

## Project Overview

London Capital is a news and content management platform that consists of a Mobile App and an Admin Panel. 
Administrators can publish news articles and blogs across different categories, while users can explore, search, 
bookmark, share, and download news directly from the mobile application. Users also receive push notifications 
whenever new content is published.
