# House Warming

## Project Overview

House Warming is a property and event management platform that allows homeowners to organize 
housewarming events and invite guests both virtually and in person. The platform consists of a Website, 
an Admin Panel, and four user panels: Homeowner, Agent, Title, and Lender. Homeowners can manage properties, 
customize invitation themes, create gift registries, and invite guests, while Agents, Title, 
and Lenders collaborate through their respective portals.
