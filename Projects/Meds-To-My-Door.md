# Meds To My Door

## Project Overview

Meds To My Door is an e-commerce pharmacy platform that enables users to browse and enquire 
about medicines online. Customers can explore medicine listings, view detailed product information, 
select the required medicine strength, add products to the cart, and submit enquiries through the website. 
The platform consists of a Website and an Admin Panel, allowing administrators to manage products,
customer enquiries, categories, salts, blogs, and FAQs efficiently.
