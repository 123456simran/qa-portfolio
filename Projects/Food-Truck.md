# Food Truck

## Project Overview

Food Truck is a food ordering and vendor management platform designed to help food truck 
owners manage their business operations. The platform consists of an Admin Panel and a Vendor Panel. 
Administrators can manage vendors and subscription plans, while vendors can manage orders, menus, products, 
and monitor sales through a dedicated dashboard.
